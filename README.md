# HDB Pricing

HDB Pricing is an independent map for exploring recent HDB resale flat prices across Singapore. It uses publicly available transaction data to make prices in the neighbourhoods you care about easier to understand.

## Why I made this

We all have a rough sense of which neighbourhoods are more expensive and which are more affordable. I wanted a clearer way to quantify those impressions.

HDB Pricing shows recent resale transactions on a map, so you can get a more grounded sense of prices in the areas you care about. Whether you are curious about your neighbourhood, helping your parents compare prices, or starting your own home-buying journey, I hope it makes public housing data more approachable.

## How it works

Browse the map, then search for a block, street, town, or another Singapore place. You can filter the results by flat type, price, and remaining lease.

Each coloured dot represents an HDB block. Its colour reflects the median price per square foot for recent matching resale transactions. Select a block to view the individual sales behind its map marker, including the registration month, flat type, floor area, storey range, total sale price, and price per square foot.

The map is designed to help you compare recent HDB resale prices by town, block, and flat type. It is not an official HDB or government service.

## Data source and limitations

The map uses HDB's public [Resale flat prices based on registration date from Jan 2017 onwards](https://data.gov.sg/datasets?topics=housing&resultId=d_8b84c4ee58e3cfc0ece0d773c8ca6abc) dataset on data.gov.sg.

Historical resale prices are indicative only. They are not an official valuation, financial advice, or a prediction of a flat's selling price. Every transaction differs based on factors such as flat type, floor area, storey, remaining lease, condition, timing, and buyer and seller circumstances.

Some blocks may have no transaction that matches your current filters. In that case, the map does not estimate a price.
